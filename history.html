January 12, 2021
Pandemonium (pandemonium.space) is officially launched by Caveman-Lawyer as a fresh 100% Vanilla anarchy server, offering pure, unclaimable survival gameplay without Factions or Land Claim plugins.
  • Core Mechanics: Combined complete anarchy with a single non-negotiable rule: No Hacking.
  • Moderation & Anti-Cheat: Deployed active moderation and high-quality anti-cheat systems specifically to eliminate client hacks and shut down intentional lag machines.
  • Player Freedom: Aside from hack prevention, zero admin interference allowed players to freely build mega-structures, grief, steal, and wage unscripted faction wars.
July 31, 2021
Pandemonium establishes itself as a staple non-hacked anarchy server, updating to Minecraft 1.16.5 and securing a long-term foundation.
  • World Guarantee: Maintained a strict promise of zero map resets, fostering a dedicated player base and player-built infrastructure.
  • Expanded Rules: Strictly banned duplication glitches alongside client hacks to preserve the legitimate survival economy.
  • Active Hub: Utilized its official Discord community to coordinate faction alliances, trade, and mega-build projects.

The 2L2B Experiment (Short-Lived Parallel Event):

During Pandemonium's multi-year run, a short-lived regional experiment named 2L2B (2l2b.mooo.com) launched as an unmoderated, Lebanon-hosted anarchy server providing offline-mode (cracked) access and low ADSL ping (~17ms).

· Due to immediate legal complications in Lebanon regarding unmoderated public chat regulations, 2L2B closed after running for only 2 days.
· Following its swift shutdown, its player base transitioned into Pandemonium's established community.
